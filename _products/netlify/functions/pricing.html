高仁提供：独立网站建设、域名和服务器支持、网站托管、商品展示、在线下单、支付接入、促销页面、配送范围设置、后台基础管理和日常维护支持。
Sorghuman provides: website build, domain & server support, hosting, product display, online ordering, payment integration, promo pages, delivery-zone setup, basic back-office management, and day-to-day maintenance support.

门店负责：提供门店资料、商品清单、价格、图片、配送范围和订单处理安排；上线后负责确认订单、备货、配送或自取。
The store is responsible for: providing store info, product list, prices, images, delivery zones, and order-handling arrangements; after launch, confirming orders, stocking, and delivery or pickup.
