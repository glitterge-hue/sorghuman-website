Who we are

A trusted bridge between China and America

Sorghuman Holding LLC is a New York-registered company specializing in the import and distribution of authentic frozen Chinese food products. We work directly with established manufacturers in China to deliver high-quality, ready-to-cook products to retailers across the northeastern U.S.
