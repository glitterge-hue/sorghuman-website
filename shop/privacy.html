Privacy Policy

—

Last updated: June 18, 2026

This store operates its online store on the Sorghuman platform, provided by Sorghuman Holding LLC ("Sorghuman"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you order online.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through our online store, we collect:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Delivery address
  • Order details and purchase history
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe; we do not store card numbers)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send order confirmation and delivery notifications via email
  • Send order status updates via SMS text message (see SMS section below)
  • Contact you regarding your order
  • Improve our store and service

3. SMS Messaging

By providing your phone number and opting in at checkout, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages related to your order — including order confirmations, delivery updates, and status notifications. These messages are sent by Sorghuman on behalf of this store.

  • Message frequency: Varies by order activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
  • To opt out: Reply STOP to any SMS message to unsubscribe. You will receive one final confirmation message.
  • To get help: Reply HELP to any SMS message for assistance.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with service providers strictly to operate the store and fulfill your order:

  • Sorghuman Holding LLC — the platform operator that powers our online ordering, delivery coordination, and notifications
  • Stripe — for payment processing (Stripe Privacy Policy)
  • Twilio — for SMS delivery notifications (Twilio Privacy Policy)
  • Delivery personnel assigned to your order

These providers act on our behalf to deliver the service and are not permitted to use your information for their own marketing.

5. Data Security

We and our platform provider implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All payment data is handled by PCI-compliant Stripe.

6. Data Retention

We retain your order information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at support@sorghuman.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: support@sorghuman.com
  • Platform operator: Sorghuman Holding LLC
