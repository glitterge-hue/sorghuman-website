Local Delivery Partner · 本地配送合伙人
📍 Your neighborhood · 本地社区 Part-time / Flexible Daily pay · 当日结算

About this role · 职位概述

Be the last-mile delivery partner for Sorghuman in your local community. When customers in your area place orders on our website, you pick up the products and deliver them — on your own schedule. Delivery fees are settled the same day. This is a flexible, part-time opportunity ideal for community-connected individuals who want to earn on their own terms.

成为公司在您所在社区的本地配送合伙人。当地顾客在网站下单后，您负责取货配送，时间灵活自主，配送费用当天结算。非常适合有社区人脉、希望灵活赚取收入的朋友。

Requirements · 任职要求

  • Reliable personal vehicle · 有私家车
  • At least one 7 cu ft chest or upright freezer at home for product storage · 家中至少有一台7立方英尺的冰柜用于存放产品
  • Printer preferred — to receive and print customer orders · 有打印机优先，可打印订单
  • Active WeChat groups in the local Chinese community · 有本地华人微信群，有社区人脉
  • Ability to promote our products within your community network · 能在华人社群中积极推广公司产品
  • Reliable, punctual, and customer-friendly · 守时可靠，服务态度好

How it works · 合作方式

  • Customers in your area place orders on sorghuman.com · 您所在地区的顾客在网站下单
  • We notify you and arrange product pickup · 我们通知您并安排取货
  • You store products in your freezer and schedule deliveries at your convenience · 产品存放在您的冰柜中，自主安排送货时间
  • Deliver to customers and confirm completion · 送货上门，确认完成
  • Delivery fee settled same day · 配送费用当天结算，无需等待
  • Promote Sorghuman in your WeChat groups and community — earn referral credit · 在微信群和社区中推广公司，额外获得推广奖励

Why partner with us · 为什么选择合作

  • No upfront investment required · 无需前期投入
  • Flexible hours — deliver on your own schedule · 时间完全自主灵活
  • Same-day pay on every delivery · 每单配送费当天结算
  • Grow with us — as the customer base in your area expands, so does your income · 随着您所在区域订单量增长，收入同步增长
  • Exclusive territory — we prioritize one partner per area · 区域优先权，同一地区优先支持一位合伙人
